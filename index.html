About Me Kerala, India
UI/UX Designer · Graphic Designer

Graphic Designer
turned
UX Thinker.

It started with branding work, logo briefs, and late nights in Illustrator. Two years of professional design experience taught me a lot about making things look right — but I kept noticing something. A beautiful interface that confuses its users isn't really beautiful at all.

That realisation moved me from graphic design into UI/UX. I started asking questions before opening any design tool. What does this person actually need? Where do they get stuck? What would make this moment less frustrating?

Today I bring a strong visual foundation into a research-first process — conducting user interviews, building personas, running design thinking frameworks, and translating real insights into interfaces that genuinely work.
